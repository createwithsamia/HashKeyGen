About HashKeyGen

Choosing the right hashtags and keywords takes time, especially for new Instagram creators. HashKeyGen is a simple and user-friendly tool that saves time by generating relevant hashtags and keywords across 20 popular Instagram categories, based on what your post is about.

🎯 Niche-based

Suggestions are matched to 20 categories and 100 sub-niches, like Gaming → Mobile Gaming.

🧠 Topic-aware

Your own words are checked, so different topics give different results.

⚡ Fast and simple
